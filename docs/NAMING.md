# Name and icon ideas

The app now covers clips, social posting, writing, graphics and a brand library, so "Sermon Clips"
undersells it. Shortlist (check trademarks and app-store availability before choosing):

| Name | Why it fits | Icon mockup |
|---|---|---|
| **Lampstand** (recommended) | Matthew 5:15: a light put on a stand so everyone in the house sees it. That is what church communications do. | `assets/icon-mockups/lampstand.svg`: the flame is a play button |
| **Herald** | Announcing good news, every channel. | `herald.svg`: open Bible with light, play button on the page |
| **Steeple Studio** | Instantly reads "church" and "creative tool". | `steeple-studio.svg`: a steeple with a play-button door inside crop marks |
| **Parable** | Stories that carry truth; every clip, post and blog is one. | `parable.svg`: an open book inside a speech bubble |
| **Inkwell** | Writing and design for the church office. | `inkwell.svg`: a design pen nib with a cross |
| **Selah** | Pause and reflect; short-form moments from Sunday. | `selah.svg`: a reel frame with a cross and caption lines |

All mockups are single-weight white lines on a transparent background, so they take the color of
whatever theme is active. To rename the app, change `APP_NAME` in `src/daemon/tools.js`,
`productName` in `package.json`, and the brand text in `src/ui/App.jsx`.
